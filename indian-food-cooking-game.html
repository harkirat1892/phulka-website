An Indian Food Cooking Game with Real Highway Soul

Step away from generic burger timers and step up to a roadside Punjabi dhaba on the Grand Trunk Road. Cook steaming phulkas, ladle tadka dal, pour chilled matka lassi, and experience the warmth of an Indian highway kitchen.

A Living Grand Trunk Road Kitchen

From morning fog in Siyaal (winter) to monsoon rain drumming on the tin roof in Barsaat, Daadi's dhaba changes with the Punjabi calendar. Truck drivers heading from Delhi to Amritsar, night-shift nurses, and college students all pull up to your counter.

Authentic Dishes, Zero Stress

Serve steel thalis loaded with puffed phulkas, yellow dal, clay-cup lassi, seasonal sabzi, kheer, and winter sarson da saag. There are no paywalls, no energy bars, and no game-over screens.

Built for Smooth 120 Hz Touch Gameplay

Every character, truck, tawa, and rising wisp of steam is drawn procedurally in code so the game downloads fast, runs at up to 120 fps, and works anywhere without internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Indian dishes can you cook in Phulka?

You start with tawa phulka rotis and dal, and unlock sweet matka lassi, seasonal sabzi, makhni butter, rice kheer, parathas, and winter sarson da saag.

Does Phulka have ads or paywalls?

No. Phulka has zero ads, zero energy timers, and zero microtransactions.
