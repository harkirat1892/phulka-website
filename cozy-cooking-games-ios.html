Cozy Cooking Game for iPhone & iPad

A cozy cooking game for iOS, with nobody left hungry

Phulka is a gentle cooking game for iPhone and iPad. You take over Daadi's little roadside dhaba on the Grand Trunk Road, puff rotis on a hot tawa, and feed the truckers, students and nurses who stop by. No ads, no accounts, and it plays offline.

What makes a cooking game cozy?

Most cooking games on the App Store are about speed: angry customers, red timers and coins you have to buy. A cozy cooking game keeps the satisfying rhythm of cooking but takes out the stress. Phulka is built that way from the start.

  • No fail state. A burnt roti goes to the crows, Daadi laughs, and you make another.
  • Kindness instead of game overs. When a guest can't pay the full bill, you take what they have or put it on the house. Nobody is turned away.
  • Short sessions. A day at the dhaba fits in a few minutes, then Daadi tells you a short story before bed.
  • Nothing to buy. Free, no ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking.

Made for iPhone and iPad

Every person, pot and roti is drawn in code and animated at up to 120 fps on ProMotion displays. It is a portrait game you can play with one thumb, and it saves to your phone, so it works on a plane, in the metro, or anywhere the signal drops.

If you like these games, try Phulka

Players who enjoy calm, story-led games about food and people, such as Venba, Unpacking, Spiritfarer or Good Pizza, Great Pizza, tend to like Phulka. It has the tactile cooking of a restaurant game and the warmth of a small story about a grandmother and her regulars.

Questions

Is Phulka free on iPhone?

Yes. Phulka is free on iPhone and iPad, with no ads and no in-app purchases. You can also join the public TestFlight beta.

Does Phulka work offline?

Yes. It never needs the internet. Your progress is saved on your device.

Is Phulka stressful?

No. Guests have patience, but there is no game over. Burnt food just gets made again, and guests who can't pay are still fed.

Which iPhones can run Phulka?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 14 or later. It runs at up to 120 fps on ProMotion displays.
