Indian & Punjabi Cooking Game

A desi cooking game that tastes like Daadi's kitchen

Phulka is an Indian cooking game about the food you grew up with: hot phulkas off the tawa, a big pot of dal, sweet lassi and, on winter days, sarson da saag. It is set in a Punjabi roadside dhaba, run by a grandmother who is practical, funny and never lets anyone leave hungry.

Real desi food, cooked the way it is

A phulka is a roti that puffs up on an open flame. In the game you cook it the way it is made at home: dough on the tawa, flip at the bubbles, then onto the flame until it balloons. Around it you serve dal, lassi, parathas, sabzi, kheer, sarson da saag and makhni as your dhaba grows.

Punjab, through its people

The culture comes through people and places, not lessons. Truckers on the Delhi to Amritsar run, a nurse coming off a night shift, students in exam week, and Daadi, who has a comment for all of them. Festival days such as Vaisakhi, Diwali and Lohri are marked on Daadi's calendar, and at night she tells you a story her own Daadi told her.

For desi families everywhere

Phulka is rated for everyone (PEGI 3, 4+), so it is easy to share with parents, kids and cousins. It is in English, plays offline, and has no ads, which makes it a good one to hand to a grandparent or a child.

Questions

Is there an Indian cooking game without ads?

Yes. Phulka has no ads, no in-app purchases and collects no data.

What food can you cook in Phulka?

Phulkas (rotis), dal and lassi from the start, then paratha, sabzi, kheer, sarson da saag and makhni as upgrades.

Is Phulka about Punjabi culture?

It is set in a Punjabi roadside dhaba on the GT Road. The culture comes through the food, the regulars and Daadi's bedtime stories.

Is Phulka good for kids?

Yes. It is rated PEGI 3 and 4+, with no violence, ads or chat.
