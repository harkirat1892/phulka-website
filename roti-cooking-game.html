An Authentic Roti & Phulka Cooking Game on the Tawa

Most cooking games treat bread as a timer bar. In Phulka, the roti is the heart of the kitchen: slap fresh whole-wheat atta onto a hot cast-iron tawa, wait for the steam bubbles to rise, flip at the golden ring, and puff each phulka over an open flame.

How the Tawa & Flame Physics Work

Every phulka goes through three tactile stages on Daadi's hearth: first side on the iron tawa until steam bubbles form, second side until brown spots set, and then straight onto the open chulha flame where it balloons into a soft, steaming orb. Leave it too long and the crows get a crispy snack.

More Than Rotis: Dal, Lassi, Sabzi & Parathas

As your roadside dhaba grows along the Grand Trunk Road, you unlock clay-pot sweet lassi, seasonal sabzi, makki di roti with sarson da saag in winter, and buttery parathas.

The Roti Jar: Pre-Paid Meals for Strangers

Regulars who love your cooking leave pre-paid rotis in the counter jar. When an ITI trainee or a stranded commuter arrives with an empty wallet, the jar feeds them—and days later, a handwritten thank-you note appears on Daadi's Wall.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you really puff rotis on the flame in Phulka?

Yes! You slap dough onto the tawa, flip when bubbles appear, and move the roti onto the open flame to puff it up before serving it on a steel thali.

Is this roti cooking game free and offline?

100% free, completely offline, with zero ads and zero in-app purchases on both iOS and Android.
