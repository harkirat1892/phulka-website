The Soul of the Game

Why Phulka is a Heartwarming Story to Play

Most cooking games test your stress limits with ticking timers and furious customers. Phulka does the opposite: it invites you into a world of comfort, generosity, and genuine human connection.

Daadi Hands Over the Tawa

After decades on the Grand Trunk Road, Daadi’s knees need a rest. She hands you the keys, her recipes, and her sharp wit. She sits right beside you on the wooden bench—offering proud smiles when your phulkas puff high, and gentle chuckles when one gets a little too crisp.

Family & Legacy

The Rule: “Nobody Leaves Hungry”

When an exhausted highway trucker has only a few coins left, or a tired student in exam week is short on cash, you don't turn them away. Daadi reminds you: “Your dhaba, your call.” Put their meal on the house. In Phulka, generosity is never penalized—it turns passersby into lifelong friends.

Pay What You Can

The Living Spirit of Seva & Langar

Rooted in the timeless Punjabi and Sikh tradition of langar—where everyone sits side by side as equals to share food—Phulka conveys culture through warmth and action, never through lectures. It is the simple, sacred joy of feeding people with unconditional love.

Punjabi Hospitality

Daadi's Regulars' Book

Every guest on the highway has a journey. Mr. Sethi who is always rushing, Bebe who wants her roti soft, the trucker doing the Delhi–Amritsar night run, and the shy kid down the road. Every visit reveals another chapter of their lives in Daadi’s handwritten book.

Unfolding Characters

Bedtime Stories Under the Stars

When the service ends, the highway quiets down, and the wooden galla is counted, Daadi invites you: “Sit with me a minute. Want to hear a story my Daadi told me?” Unwind each evening with sweet, 1-minute Punjabi folklore and heartwarming bedtime tales.

Evening Rituals

Pure, Peaceful Craftsmanship

No obnoxious video ads interrupting your cooking. No timers forcing you to pay real money. No analytics or tracking your phone. Phulka is built with honest hand-drawn art, authentic acoustic instruments, and a silky 120 FPS engine that runs completely offline.

Respect for Players

“Some customers can't pay the full bill. Take what they have, or put it on the house. Nobody leaves hungry.”

In Phulka, going broke is impossible. Kindness is an active choice with no penalties—only full bellies, grateful smiles, and warm memories.

A Day at the Dhaba

Cooking with Rhythm, Rhythm with Heart

Master the satisfying rhythm of authentic roadside cooking. Simple, tactile, and deeply satisfying to play on touchscreens.

1

Slap, Flip & Puff

Drop dough on the hot cast-iron tawa. Watch the blisters rise, flip it at the exact moment, then hold it over the open flame until it puffs into a round, golden phulka. Watch the clock—or the crows will get lunch!

2

Ladle Dal & Pour Lassi

Keep the big degh of dal simmering with fresh tadka. Pour chilled sweet lassi into earthenware glasses, and assemble complete, steaming thalis with a generous pat of golden homemade butter.

3

Handle Highway Rushes

When interstate passenger buses pull over, a dozen travelers walk up at once. Prioritize orders, keep your cool, and drag the right dishes to the right customers before their patience slips away.

4

Upgrade the Dhaba

Count today's galla and invest your honest earnings back into the dhaba. Hang warm fairy lights, add wooden charpais, bring in a second tawa, or buy a cooler matka for ice-cold lassi.
