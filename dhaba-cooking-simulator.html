Dhaba Simulator on the GT Road

A dhaba cooking simulator on the Grand Trunk Road

Run a real roadside dhaba: the tawa, the dal degh, the lassi matka and the galla. Phulka puts you behind Daadi's counter on the GT Road, where trucks, buses and regulars pull in all day long.

How the dhaba runs

  • The tawa. Slap the dough on, flip it when the bubbles come, and puff it over the flame. Leave it too long and it burns.
  • The counter. Drag rotis, dal and lassi to the right guest before their patience runs out.
  • The bus rush. When a passenger bus stops, a dozen people want lunch at once. Plan your tawa space.
  • The galla. Count the day's takings in the evening and spend them on the dhaba.

Grow your dhaba

Each evening you choose upgrades: fairy lights over the awning, another charpai, a bigger tawa, a lassi matka, and new dishes such as paratha, sabzi, kheer, sarson da saag and makhni. New dishes bring new orders, so the kitchen gets busier as the dhaba gets better known.

The regulars

A dhaba is its regulars. Mr. Sethi is always in a hurry, Bebe wants her roti soft, and the truckers on the night run know Daadi by name. Their stories fill Daadi's Regulars' Book as they keep coming back.

Daadi has one rule: nobody leaves hungry. Some guests can't pay the full bill. You decide whether to take what they have or put it on the house, and kindness is never punished.

Questions

What is a dhaba?

A dhaba is a roadside restaurant on Indian highways, famous for fresh rotis, dal and lassi served to truck drivers and travelers. The Grand Trunk Road through Punjab is lined with them.

Is Phulka a restaurant simulator?

It is a cozy cooking and time-management game. You cook, serve, manage rushes and upgrade the dhaba, without the stress of failing.

Can I play the dhaba simulator on Android?

Yes. Phulka is on Google Play for Android 7.0 and up, and on iPhone and iPad.

Does it cost anything?

No. It is free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
