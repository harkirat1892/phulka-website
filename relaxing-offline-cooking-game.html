A Relaxing Offline Cooking Game With Zero Ads or Paywalls

Most mobile cooking games are designed to stress you out so you buy gems. Phulka was built as the exact opposite: a calm, tactile roadside dhaba you can open on a flight, a commute, or a quiet evening with zero internet and zero ads.

No Game Over, No Gem Timers

If a roti burns, the highway crows get a treat and you simply slap another ball of atta on the tawa. If you run out of money for morning supplies, Daadi quietly leaves a fresh sack of atta in the kitchen so you can always keep cooking.

Ambient Highway Soundscape

Listen to the gentle crackle of the chulha flame, distant GT Road trucks, monsoon raindrops, and Daadi's dry, affectionate encouragement—or toggle sound off anytime in Settings.

Complete Privacy on Your Phone

Phulka collects zero data, requires no login, and stores your dhaba's progress locally on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Phulka work on airplane mode?

Yes. Every sound, story, and day at the dhaba runs 100% offline on your phone.

Are there any hidden in-app purchases?

None at all. Every upgrade—fairy lights, bigger tawa, lassi matka, charpai—is earned with the rupees you make at the dhaba counter.
